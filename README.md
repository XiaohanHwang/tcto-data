## Download notes

- **AmazonEmployee:** download `train.csv`. Kaggle may require sign-in and acceptance of the competition rules; the CatBoost mirror provides an alternative download.
- **SpectF:** use `SPECTF.train` and the corrected `SPECTF.test` in the archive.
- **ALBERT / Newsgroups:** the labeled challenge training data are in `*_train.data` and `*_train.solution`.

Please cite the original dataset sources and follow their respective usage terms.
